Privacy Policy for HouseLearning

 

Last updated: October 22, 2026

 

This Privacy Policy describes how HouseLearning ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, retains, and discloses information when you use our websites, educational content, games, SafeLibrary, accounts, dashboards, community features, feedback tools, and related products and services (collectively, the "Service"). Where applicable law requires consent for optional processing, we request that consent separately. This Policy does not limit any rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

 
 

1. Interpretation and Definitions

 

For the purposes of this Policy:

 
       
  • **Service** refers to the HouseLearning website, including all educational lessons, practice problems, projects, personalized account features (like the **Student/Teacher Dashboard**), and the **HouseLearning Community Forum**.
    •    
  • **Personal Data** is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
    •    
  • **Usage Data** refers to data collected automatically, either generated by the use of the Service or from the Service infrastructure itself (e.g., the duration of a page visit).
    •    
  • **Cookies** are small files placed on your computer, mobile device, or any other device by a website, containing details of your browsing history on that website.
    •    
  • **Data Controller** is the legal entity that determines the purposes and means of the processing of Personal Data (in this case, HouseLearning).
    •  
 
 

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

   

Account and Educational Data (Student/Teacher Dashboard)

 

When you create an account, log in (using **Firebase Authentication**), or use the features of the Service that require identification, we collect the following types of Personal Data:

 
       
  • **Account Registration Information:** Your name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. We use **Google Firebase Authentication** to manage your account, provide secure login, and communicate with you about the Service. **Legal Basis: Contractual Necessity.**
    •    
  • **Educational/Progress Data:** Information viewed on the **Dashboard**, including lesson completion status, practice problem scores, project submissions, assignments, and any feedback received. This data is stored using **Firebase Realtime Database (RTDB)** and is essential to provide personalized features like adaptive practice and allow teachers (if applicable) to monitor and support your learning. **Legal Basis: Contractual Necessity.**
    •  
 

Forum Content and Activity Data

 

When you use the **HouseLearning Community Forum** to post, comment, or start a discussion, we collect and process the following:

 
       
  • **User-Generated Content:** The text, images, or other materials you intentionally submit, including posts, comments, and private messages. This content is generally **publicly viewable** on the forum.
    •    
  • **Activity Data:** Your display name/username, the time and date of your activity, and any metadata associated with your contributions (e.g., the post tag you select).
    •  
 

We use this data to operate the forum, facilitate community interaction, and enforce our usage policies. **Legal Basis: Contractual Necessity and Legitimate Interest.**

 

Feedback Tool Data (Voluntary Submission)

 

A feedback or survey interface may be presented while you use the Service. If you submit a response, we collect the information you choose to provide:

 
       
  • **Submission Content:** Text comments, the selected 5-point rating (smiley), and optional screenshots you choose to upload.
    •    
  • Technical and activity data: The submission time, page URL or path, browser user-agent, a pseudonymous identifier or account identifier, and a snapshot that may include locally counted page views, clicks, scroll depth, time on page, and features used.
    •    
  • **Purpose:** This data is used strictly for improving our Service, fixing bugs, and enhancing the user experience. This data is stored in **Google Cloud Firestore**.
    •  
 

Important: While we aim to protect all submitted data, please ensure that you redact or avoid including any Personal Data (such as real names, private student records, or passwords) within the text or images of any voluntary screenshot submissions. **Legal Basis: Legitimate Interest.**

 

Usage, Diagnostic Data, and Tracking Technologies

 

Our hosting, analytics, authentication, and other service providers may automatically process technical and usage information, such as IP address, browser and device information, pages and URLs requested, timestamps, referring pages, and diagnostic data. URLs or search terms may contain information you enter; please avoid putting sensitive information in URLs or site searches.

 

Some site scripts also store preferences and identifiers in browser storage. Depending on the features you use, this may include cookie preferences, language and display settings, feedback-window settings, a random anonymous feedback identifier, and a locally maintained page-view count. These values generally remain until they expire, are deleted by the Service, or you clear your browser storage.

 

Operational diagnostics may record a page URL and timestamp once for each distinct page URL. A diagnostic script may also record that developer tools were opened, along with an account identifier or anonymous status; while developer tools are open, it may capture up to 300 characters of console output. We use these diagnostics for site security, troubleshooting, and investigating suspected misuse. Depending on the processing and applicable law, our legal basis may be our legitimate interests in securing and operating the Service. Do not place passwords, personal records, or other sensitive information in URLs or the browser console.

 

We use the following categories of cookies and similar technologies:

 
       
  • Essential technologies: Used for core functions such as authentication, security, and saving choices. Depending on the processing and applicable law, our legal basis may be contractual necessity or legitimate interests in providing and securing the Service.
    •    
  • Analytics technologies: Google Analytics 4 loads on pages using our analytics integration. Google Consent Mode v2 starts with analytics and advertising storage denied. In this state, the tag may send cookieless measurement requests, including page and interaction information; denied storage prevents Google Analytics from using analytics cookies but does not prevent those requests. If you allow analytics storage in cookie preferences, the analytics storage signal is updated to granted. Requests may include page paths and titles, referrer paths, device/browser information, and network information such as an IP address processed by Google. We remove query strings and fragments from page-view URLs. This description is not a representation that analytics data is anonymous.
    •    
  • Marketing technologies: A small number of embedded games load Google AdSense only if you enable marketing in cookie preferences. Google may use cookies or similar technologies and process online identifiers and device/browser information to deliver and measure ads under its own privacy terms. Withdrawing marketing consent prevents the advertising script from loading on subsequent page views; if it was already loaded, the page reloads to stop further execution.
    •

Enhanced Measurement is enabled for these streams. Depending on the page, browser, and available content, it can automatically collect:

Your data stored in connection with this service is subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement. We strive to only use the GitHub API for necessary operational functions that do not require complex, persistent database storage.

 

We **do not share** Personal Data with third parties for their direct marketing purposes.

 
 

4. Data Retention

 

In the SafeLibrary GA4 property, event-level data retention is set to 2 months and user-level data retention is set to 14 months. The setting to reset the retention period for a user identifier when new activity occurs is enabled. These are GA4 data-retention settings; they do not set the retention period for browser cookies, Firebase records, GitHub-hosted content, or copies held by other providers.

 

The cookie-preference cookie is configured to expire after up to 365 days; this is separate from the GA4 retention periods. Browser local-storage values generally remain until cleared or removed. Account and educational data are retained while needed to provide the Service and manage the account. Forum content may remain publicly available until removed under our moderation or deletion processes. Feedback and operational records are retained as needed for the purposes described above; a fixed deletion schedule is not currently specified for all such records. We will delete or anonymize personal information when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.
